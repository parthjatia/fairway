# M16: Fairway Launch pivot

**New positioning:** "Fairway Launch: fair token launches on Solana. Known snipers pay more, humans pay less."
**Pitch line:** "Other launchpads tax everyone who's early. Fairway taxes snipers."

## Rules
- Never ask questions. Decide, log the decision in DECISIONS.md, continue.
- Reuse the existing engine: scoring, onchain markout settlement, pools, tiers, Streams pipeline, frontend.
- Keep the SOL/USDC features working. They become secondary evidence ("the engine also works on liquid pairs").
- Do NOT regenerate existing fixtures with a new seed. Add new fixture files for launches.
- Work in the order below. **After each numbered step: run tests, then `git commit`.** That way, if time runs out, there's always a working, demoable state.
- Add an "M16 Launch pivot" section to PROGRESS.md with one checkbox per step, and tick them as you go.

## Steps (in priority order)

1. **Onchain launch fee schedule**
   - Per-pool launch params: `launch_slot`, `window_slots`, plus a per-tier fee curve (start fee, end fee).
   - Fee = f(tier, slots since launch). Defaults (in config):
     - TOXIC: 1000 bps, decaying to 80 bps
     - STANDARD / fresh wallets: 300 bps, decaying to 30 bps (so fresh-wallet snipers don't escape)
     - RETAIL: 30 bps, decaying to 5 bps (low from block 0)
   - After the window, the normal tier fees apply.
   - Tests: the decay math, the window boundary, a fresh wallet at block 0, the wrong-signer score attack still blocked.

2. **Onchain pool TWAP for tokens without an oracle**
   - Add a price accumulator (price × slots) to Pool, updated on every swap.
   - `settle_markout` uses the pool TWAP over a short window ending at trade slot + 75 when the pool has no oracle configured. Pools with a Pyth oracle keep using Pyth.
   - Tests: TWAP correctness, no-oracle path, settle-too-early does nothing.
   - Document the manipulation risk (an attacker moving the price around t+75 to distort someone's markout) and the mitigations (TWAP window, cost of moving the price, shrinkage) in RISKS.md.

3. **Launch simulation (make it the default /backtest view)**
   - Many simulated launches with snipers, bundlers (several wallets funded by one), retail buyers and good bots (DCA-style).
   - Compare three regimes: flat fee vs. time-decay-only anti-sniper fee (what other launchpads do) vs. Fairway (tier × time).
   - Metrics: sniper/bundler share of early supply, retail average entry price vs. fair price, creator/LP fee revenue, protocol revenue, and how often humans paid the high early fee.
   - The key chart: humans pay far less than under time-decay-only, while snipers pay as much or more.

4. **Launch flow and UI**
   - Reframe the landing page around fair launches. Keep the honest "Simulated data" badge in mock mode.
   - `/launch`: a create-token wizard (name, symbol, supply, starting liquidity, launch window) that works in mock mode and in local mode with the SDK.
   - `/launch/[id]`: a live launch view with the buyers list (tier, fee paid, onchain-verified vs. bootstrap score), a "sniper share of supply" meter, and a "Same buy, two wallets" panel (Retail Rita vs. a known sniper, fee in $).
   - Keep `/swap`, `/wallet`, `/pool`, `/backtest` working. In the nav, launches come first, the liquid-pair pool is secondary.

5. **Streams: score real snipers from Pump.fun history**
   - Add a filter for Pump.fun / PumpSwap swaps (program IDs marked VERIFY, with a note on how to check them) that extracts early buys and sells per launch.
   - Use it to bootstrap sniper scores across launches: wallets that repeatedly buy in the first seconds and gain right after score TOXIC.
   - Fixture tests. Update `streams/STREAM_CONFIG.md` with the exact settings and why.

6. **Docs**
   - **README:** new headline and "For judges" section (criteria table + per-prize checklists with links), the launch economics, how Solana is used, Stream config.
   - **README "What's next" section, include:**
     - Oracle-anchored pricing for liquid pairs (the pool re-centres on Pyth, so it doesn't rely on arbitrage for price accuracy)
     - Jupiter integration (quoting needs the trader's tier, since fees are per wallet)
     - Launchpad partnerships: graduating tokens land in Fairway pools
     - Scores as a service: other DEXes read Fairway tiers via `getFeeTier`
     - ZK proofs of trading history, to remove trust in the bootstrap scores
     - A sponsorship cap for the treasury that pays first-swap rent
   - **ECONOMICS.md:** launch economics, plus who benefits: creators (fairer distribution, healthier holder base), retail buyers (fair entry), LPs/creators (fee revenue from snipers), protocol (a launch fee and a share of swap fees).
   - **RISKS.md:** TWAP manipulation, fresh-wallet Sybils at launch, bundlers, unverified program IDs, memecoin perception.
   - **PITCH.md:** a new 5-minute script and Q&As, including:
     - "How is this different from existing anti-sniper fees?"
     - "Where does the price come from without arbitrage?"
     - "Why would anyone launch here instead of Pump.fun?"
     - "Can snipers just use fresh wallets?"
     - "Why won't bots go to other DEXes?" and "Where does the liquidity come from?"
   - **DEMO_SCRIPT.md:** a shot-by-shot 2–3 minute script built around a live launch.

7. **Final check**
   - `pnpm test`, `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint` all pass. Every page returns 200 in mock mode.
   - The README matches what is actually built (no overclaims).
   - Commit, and update PROGRESS.md.
